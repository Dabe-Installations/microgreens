Wheatgrass

Hard red wheat on hemp mats

Step by step

1

Soak the seed

Weigh 75–90 g of dry seed per 15x30 cm tray. Rinse until the water runs clear. For extra mould protection, rinse once in 3% hydrogen peroxide. Soak in cool water for 8–12 hours, then drain and rinse again. Pre-sprouting in a jar for another 12–24 hours gives faster, more even growth.

2

Prep the hemp mat

Cut the mat to 15x30 cm to fit the tray with no holes. Soak it until it's fully wet, then tip out the extra water. It should be damp all through but not sitting in a puddle.

3

Sow thick and even

Spread the seed in a single dense layer, edge to edge, with no piles or bare patches. Press it gently into the mat and mist well.

4

Blackout and weight (2–4 days)

Cover with an upside-down tray and put 1–2 kg on top (a bag of rice or a water bottle works). The weight pushes the roots down into the mat and gives a stronger, more even stand. Mist 1–2 times a day. Lift the cover to check for dry spots or mould.

5

Uncover and give light

When the shoots are 2–5 cm tall and pushing the lid up, remove the cover. Give 12–16 hours of light a day at 18–24°C. They'll be yellow at first and turn green within a day.

6

Water from the bottom

Once the roots have grabbed the mat, stop watering from the top. Pour a little water (about 100–150 ml) into the bottom tray each day, just enough for the mat to soak up in an hour or so. Tip out anything left standing. Hemp holds a lot of water, so it needs less than soil. Keep a fan on low.

7

Harvest

Cut at day 8–12 when the grass is 15–20 cm tall, before a second blade splits off (after that it turns bitter and woody). Use clean, sharp scissors and cut just above the mat. Harvest dry: skip watering for about 12 hours before cutting.

8

Store and clean up

Store unwashed and dry in a sealed container lined with paper towel in the fridge. Keeps 5–7 days. The used mat and roots go straight into the compost. Wash and sanitise trays before the next batch.

Troubleshooting

Grey/slimy fuzz, bad smell

Cause: Mould: too wet, too warm, poor airflow, dirty seed

Fix: Remove badly affected trays. Water less, add a fan, rinse seed better, use the peroxide rinse.

White fuzz on roots

Cause: Usually root hairs (healthy)

Fix: Mist it. Root hairs collapse and disappear; mould doesn't.

Patchy, uneven growth

Cause: Seed sown unevenly or mat dried out in blackout

Fix: Spread seed edge to edge, weight the cover, mist daily in blackout.

Pale, floppy grass

Cause: Not enough light or uncovered too late

Fix: Increase light hours or move closer to the light. Uncover at 2–5 cm.

Bitter or tough taste

Cause: Harvested too late (second blade)

Fix: Cut earlier, at 15–20 cm.

Roots lifting off mat

Cause: No weight in blackout, or mat too dry

Fix: Weight the cover and keep the mat damp.

Sour smell from tray

Cause: Water sitting in the bottom tray

Fix: Tip out leftover water. Only add what the mat absorbs.

Key tips

  • Damp, not soggy. Overwatering is the number one cause of failed wheatgrass.
  • Airflow matters more in warm weather. Run a small fan on low from uncovering to harvest.
  • Fuzzy white root hairs are normal. Mist to check before throwing a tray out.
  • Harvest before the second blade appears for the sweetest taste.
  • Write down sow date, seed weight and yield for each tray so you can compare batches.

You'll need

  • Hard red wheat seed
  • Hemp mats
  • 15x30 cm tray with no holes
  • 15x30 cm tray with holes (or a lid tray)
  • 1–2 kg weight
  • Spray bottle
  • LED grow light or a bright window
  • Small fan
  • Clean scissors or knife
  • 3% hydrogen peroxide (optional)
